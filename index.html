Coastal Road Trip Route

  • Begin in Melbourne and ease into the scenic coastal roads of Gippsland.
  • Pause at seaside cafés and lookout points for a relaxed coffee break.
  • Continue through the shoreline charm of the NSW coast and hidden beach towns.
  • Enjoy a slower detour to Jervis Bay for turquoise water and coastal calm.
  • Arrive in Sydney with the harbour in view and a long, easy final stretch behind you.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Melbourne to Gippsland

AM: Leave Melbourne and drive east through the scenic Gippsland route.
PM: Stop for a long lunch at a seaside café, explore a coastal lookout, and enjoy a relaxed beach walk.
Night: Stay in a coastal Airbnb or holiday park cabin in Gippsland, with a simple beachside setup and sunset views.
Day 2

Gippsland to Canberra

AM: Have a slow breakfast and continue inland through rolling countryside.
PM: Arrive in Canberra, visit Lake Burley Griffin, and stop at a relaxed café for lunch.
Night: Stay in a moderate-cost Airbnb or serviced apartment in Canberra near the lakefront or city centre.
Day 3

Canberra to Jervis Bay

AM: Drive toward the coast and stop for coffee in a small town along the way.
PM: Spend the afternoon at Jervis Bay beach, swimming, walking, and enjoying ocean-view dining.
Night: Stay in a family-friendly holiday park cabin or Airbnb close to the beach for a relaxed coastal night.
Day 4

Jervis Bay to Batemans Bay

AM: Start with a slow morning and scenic coastal drive north along the NSW shoreline.
PM: Stop at lookout points, beachside cafés, and small harbour towns before reaching Batemans Bay.
Night: Opt for a budget-friendly Airbnb or holiday park stay in Batemans Bay with easy beach access and simple comforts.
Day 5

Batemans Bay to Sydney

AM: Enjoy a relaxed breakfast and one last scenic stop before heading toward Sydney.
PM: Arrive in Sydney and spend the afternoon exploring harbour-side cafés or a beachside neighbourhood.
Night: Finish with a moderate-cost Airbnb or holiday park stay in Sydney near Manly, Bondi, or the harbour for easy city access.
