Coastal Road Trip Route

  • Begin in Melbourne and ease into the scenic coastal roads of Gippsland.
  • Pause at seaside cafés and lookout points for a relaxed coffee break.
  • Continue through the shoreline charm of the NSW coast and hidden beach towns.
  • Enjoy a slower detour to Jervis Bay for turquoise water and coastal calm.
  • Arrive in Sydney with the harbour in view and a long, easy final stretch behind you.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Melbourne to Gippsland

Start in Melbourne and take the scenic eastbound route toward Gippsland. Enjoy a relaxed brunch in a local café, stop at coastal lookouts, and rest in a charming seaside town for the night.

Day 2

Gippsland to Canberra

Drive through open countryside and stop for coffee in regional towns. Spend the afternoon in Canberra with lakeside walks, gardens, and a slow evening before heading further south.

Day 3

Canberra to Jervis Bay

Continue toward Jervis Bay, one of the most beautiful coastal stretches on the route. Take time for beach walks, swim breaks, and a scenic café stop overlooking the water.

Day 4

Jervis Bay to Batemans Bay

Keep the pace easy as you travel north along the NSW coast. Visit cliff-top views, local coffee shops, and quiet coves before settling in Batemans Bay for the evening.

Day 5

Batemans Bay to Sydney

On your final day, enjoy one last coastal café stop and scenic lookout before arriving in Sydney. End the trip with harbour views and a relaxed arrival into the city.
